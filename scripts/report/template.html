DIAMOND LEDGER

Two independent models, five leagues, every pick graded straight-up, against the spread, and at a flat $100 stake.

The Ledger — stats & screens The Second Opinion — category vote Trend & blowout checks
How to read this. Every bet is flat $100 at the odds stored on pick day — never re-fetched, never closing lines. Moneyline profit uses standard American-odds payout; against-the-spread bets default to −110 when no price was stored. Premier League is graded moneyline-only against a 3-way market (Home / Draw / Away) — there's no spread. This page is currently showing illustrative sample data in the exact shape the real pipeline produces; it will reflect real graded slates once data/<league>/*.json has scored games in it.
