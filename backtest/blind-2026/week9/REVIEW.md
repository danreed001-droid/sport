- **NCAAB big underdogs are not the problem.** Of the 10-19-1, the 25+ point underdogs went
  5-7-1, close to how all 25+ NCAAB underdogs did (13-16). The rest is scattered: lines of
  5-14.5 went 1-6 and favorites of 25+ went 0-2. The usual case for a big college underdog (slow
  pace, a short bench, the favorite clearing its bench) went about 4-4. It's no edge and no
  bias, and the market already prices it.
- **The forced side leans to the underdog every week:**
  - 146 of 249 clean forced sides were underdogs (59%; NCAAB 21 of 30);
  - the projection sat on the underdog's side of the line in 153 games and on the favorite's side
    in 83;
  - the average projection leaned to the underdog in every week (-0.3, -0.1, -0.6, -0.1, -1.0
    points).

  The underdog side has gone 43.1% against 52.5% on favorites. That gap isn't significant
  (p≈0.15), and forced underdogs track the all-games underdog rate week by week (Wk5-9: 55, 67,
  53, 39, 33% vs 58, 55, 49, 43, 40%). It costs a couple of points of hit rate at most. Correct it
  by not defaulting to the dog, not by defaulting to the favorite.
- **The small-line underdog cell (19-36) is the sharpest number, but it was found by looking.**
  It went 16-22 in Wk5-8 and 3-14 this week, in a week when underdogs as a whole covered 40%.
  The safe reading is "no read" in a near pick'em, not "take the favorite".
