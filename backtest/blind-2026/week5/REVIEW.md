## Misses by 7+ against the line (no strong picks this week)

- **Texas A&M +3.5 at Missouri, L 6-27 (-17.5, contaminated).** Misled by the
  money-vs-tickets split toward A&M and by Missouri's two missing running backs. The line
  never moved toward A&M, and Missouri's one healthy back was enough. A&M scored 6.
- **Pittsburgh -3.5 vs North Carolina, L 17-21 (-7.5, contaminated).** Misled by Pitt's
  4-1 ATS and home-cover streak, all built with the injured starter, and by calling the drop
  to -3.5 after his ACL tear an "overreaction". Backup Geriner threw two interceptions in his
  first start (per the contamination note).
- **Tennessee +7 vs Houston, L 3-26 (-16).** Faded a 3.5-to-7.5 move because "no injury
  reason was found", and leaned on Houston's 0-4 ATS record and a 37.5 total. Houston's
  defense (called "bottom-five" in a bullet) held Tennessee to 187 yards and sacked Ward
  four times or more. Stroud threw for 261 and ran for a score.
- **Las Vegas +3.5 at New England, L 17-45 (-24.5, contaminated).** Misled by the Raiders'
  4-0 ATS start and New England's two missing corners. The line had already fallen from 8 to
  3.5, so the value was gone, and Jeanty was questionable.
- **New Orleans +1.5 vs Minnesota, L 13-23 (-8.5).** Misled by "Jefferson and Addison
  questionable" and by regression talk about Minnesota's +5 turnover margin. The reasoning
  itself said "if Jefferson plays fully this is a pass". He played (6-79, TD), Minnesota's
  defense held the Saints to 142 yards, and New Orleans's only touchdown came on a fumble
  return.

## Patterns

- **Projections weren't sharper than the line.** Across 34 games the average miss was
  10.6 points against the projection and 10.7 against the line. On the 22 picks the stated
  edge averaged 2.9 points, and the realized edge was +0.5. The picked side came in 2-3 points
  below its projection whether it was a favorite (-3.2), an underdog (-1.8), home (-2.9) or
  road (-1.8): overconfidence, with no favorite/underdog bias.
- **Betting with a big move already made (3+ points) went 1-4 (clean 1-3).** Iowa State
  (line 14.5 to 9.5), Jacksonville (4.5 to 7.5) and Alabama (4.5-point swing) lost.
  Las Vegas (8 to 3.5) lost but is contaminated. Notre Dame (35.5 to 39.5) won.
  Fading a 4-point move (Tennessee) lost by 16. Each time the injury news that caused the
  move was then listed again as support for the same side.
- **Reverse line movement (a move against 60%+ of tickets) toward an underdog went 3-0
  clean:** Oklahoma, Kentucky, Tampa Bay. Texas A&M (contaminated) lost. The same signal
  toward a favorite went 0-2: Alabama (clean) and the Rams (contaminated).
- **Team ATS records didn't help.** Backing teams with hot ATS records and fading teams with
  cold ones went 3-7 (clean 1-4):
  - lost: Browns 6-1, Saints 8-2, Jaguars 9-2 as a favorite, Pitt 4-1, Raiders 4-0, fade of
    Texans 0-4, fade of Eagles 0-4;
  - won: Notre Dame, UCLA, fade of Washington.

  Tampa Bay won *against* its 0-13 ATS run.
- **"A low total or bad weather makes the points worth more" went 3-4 on underdog picks.**
  - lost: Browns, Titans, Iowa State (pace), Texas A&M;
  - won: Oklahoma, Kentucky, Iowa.

  The total is already in the spread, so this isn't a separate reason.
- **[angle] calls:**
  - Helped: the rest/travel edge (Steelers on 10 days vs. Colts back from London, won),
    USC's run game against Penn State's 133rd-ranked run defense (won outright), and the
    UCLA run-game mismatch (contaminated).
  - Hurt: the Browns' rest edge (lost), the Eagles' offensive-line turmoil (Philadelphia
    covered anyway), and Minnesota "regression" (lost).
- **Passes.** Passes sat within about 1 point of the line. Their results swung wildly
  (Florida -30.5 vs the line, Kansas State -21.5, Vanderbilt -25.5, Green Bay +25.5,
  Arizona -18.5), which is a reminder that a 1-3 point projected edge is small next to
  10-point game noise.
- **Process.**
  - 13 games were contaminated by search summaries: Wikipedia season pages, recap
    headlines, a live blog, and later articles surfacing in injury-status searches.
  - One clean pick (New Orleans) was locked while its own stated pass condition, Jefferson's
    status, was unresolved.
