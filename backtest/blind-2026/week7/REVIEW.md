## Question 1: is the threshold so strict that the method makes almost no picks?

**Yes.** Picks fell from 22 of 32 games in Week 5 to 6 of 23 in Week 6 and 5 of 83 in Week 7.
Most of that isn't the threshold. The projections now sit almost on the line:

- The average unshaded gap between projection and line was 0.9 points this week (NFL 0.6,
  CFB 1.2, NBA 0.9). It was 2.0 in Week 5 and about 1.9 in Week 6.
- 14 projections were exactly on the number.
- Only one game (Sacramento) reached the 4-point unshaded bar.
- Three of the five picks were made below the bar (unshaded gaps of 2 to 3.5) on market
  signals.

**Bigger unshaded edges didn't do better.** This compares the forced side by the size of
|projected margin + home spread|. Week 6 projections were recorded already shaded, so they are
doubled here.

| Unshaded edge | All | Clean |
|---|---|---|
| 0-0.5 | 26-26 | 17-15 |
| 1-1.5 | 19-19-1 | 14-13-1 |
| 2-2.5 | 14-10 | 9-5 |
| 3-3.5 | 5-7-1 | 1-5-1 |

- The biggest quarter of edges within each week went 19-13-1 (clean 12-11-1), against 51-53-1
  (clean 33-31-1) for the rest.
- The correlation between the projection's edge and the actual cover margin was 0.05 (clean,
  89 games).
- Average miss: 10.59 points for the projection and 10.57 for the line (clean).
- The Week 6 "4+ went 5-1" sign is now 4-4 clean.

**The projections show no edge.** The bar works as a filter that makes few picks, which costs
nothing because there's no edge to give up. This week the passes' forced sides went 43-35
(clean 29-22) and the picks went 2-3. It doesn't sort good sides from bad.

  - NFL home teams finished 6.1 points worse than projected (road teams covered 11 of 14);
  - CFB home teams finished 7.5 points better (14 of 17 covered);
  - NBA home teams finished 1.8 points worse.
  - Favorites came in 0.6 (NFL), 2.4 (CFB) and 0.9 (NBA) points better than projected, which
    reverses the Week 6 favorite shortfall.
  - No total projections were recorded, so the totals can't be checked.
- **The favorite / underdog lean didn't hold.**
  - Forced sides on the favorite went 12-8 this week (clean 10-6) after 7-13-2 in Weeks 5-6.
    The Weeks 5-7 clean total is 14-16-2, about a coin flip.
  - Forced sides on the underdog went 51-45 over three weeks (clean 31-26).

  one pattern that held every week. NFL forced sides on the road team went 11-1-1 clean over
  Weeks 5-7, and home forced sides went 3-8-1. NFL's 14-9-2 clean forced-side record comes from
  that and nothing else.
